      Strategy (researched Oct 18)
      • Centers: only Jokic and Wembanyama are worth a reach, with Sabonis alone in the next tier. After him, ESPN's center tiers (Sengun/Towns, Duren/Davis/Adebayo, Allen/Reid/Okongwu) are close, so you can wait. Still get a second C-eligible player by the middle rounds: good centers run out first, so the centers left late are much weaker than the guards and forwards left (the 20th-best C projects about 34 points a game, vs 36–38 at other positions).
      • Point guard is the deepest position. Don't reach for one. Small forward goes fast at the top (Barnes, Amen Thompson, Flagg), then deep in the middle.
      • Games played count. In H2H points every game is points, so a 45-ppg player who misses 25 games is worth less than a 40-ppg player who plays all 82. Value here is season points over a replacement player. Early on a missed game counts as zero: your first picks have to play. The injury penalty eases off round by round, so by the last rounds a fragile star who scores a lot when healthy is the upside pick (the board re-sorts for the round of your next pick).
      • One injured stash max: there's one IR slot. While he's out a free agent fills in, so his value here counts only the games after ESPN's estimated return. At most two injury-prone players in all (missed 40%+ of games over three seasons, or out 30+ days): one in IR, one on the bench, so you never have to drop someone to make room.
      • Durability counts: projected games blend ESPN with the last three seasons of games played, and players who miss a lot of time take a risk discount. Fragile stars still show up when they slide far enough, just later.
      • Two-position players get a small bonus: daily lineups are easier to fill. A tiebreaker, not a reason to reach.
      • Late-round values: Kessler, Kel'el Ware, Quickley, Podziemski, Jaquez Jr., Gui Santos.
      • Daily lineups: lineups lock at each tip-off. Streaming players with more games that week wins matchups.
